Detailed environment & level scope
---
01  Route & greybox
---
Circuit planning, 10-car grid spacing, road widths, turns, junctions and sightline review.
---
02  Road & surface work
---
Road mesh, kerbs, sidewalks, lane markings, medians, drains and pavement transitions.
---
03  Buildings & city assets
---
Modular small and large buildings, shopfronts, roof forms, windows, doors and skyline masses.
---
04  Race dressing
---
Start / finish art, direction boards, barriers, spectator zones, street furniture and vegetation.
---
05  Materials & lighting
---
Road and building materials, restrained night lighting, selected lit windows and visual consistency.
---
06  Final level package
---
Scene organisation, reusable assets, visual cleanup, agreed refinements and handover notes.
---
4-month production schedule
---
MONTH 01   /   Planning & sample
---
Approve the visual target. Prepare the loop map, road greybox, 10-car grid and a sample street.Review: route and quality direction.
---
MONTH 02   /   City construction
---
Build road sections, mixed-height building modules, junctions and district boundaries.Review: complete route and main structures.
---
MONTH 03   /   Detail & night lighting
---
Dress all urban sectors with materials, shops, barriers, African spectators and street lighting.Review: full environment presentation.
---
MONTH 04   /   Refinement & handover
---
Resolve agreed visual feedback, clean up assets, organise scenes and prepare the level package.Review: final environment delivery.
